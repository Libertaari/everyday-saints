About Everyday Saints

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

What Is Everyday Saints?

Everyday Saints is an independent website dedicated to helping people discover content related to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, its teachings, history, culture, and people.

There are countless videos, podcasts, articles, and other resources available online, created by individuals and organizations with a wide range of interests, experiences, and perspectives. Finding them, however, isn't always easy.

Everyday Saints aims to make that discovery easier by bringing content from multiple sources together in one convenient place.

Whether you're looking to learn something new, explore a familiar subject more deeply, or simply see what others are discussing, we hope you'll find something worthwhile here.

Why the Name Everyday Saints?

In the New Testament, the word saints refers to followers of Jesus Christ. It isn't a title reserved exclusively for extraordinary individuals or people who have achieved perfection.

Latter-day Saints understand that discipleship is a lifelong process. We are ordinary people, with ordinary lives, striving to follow Jesus Christ, learn from our experiences, and become better along the way.

That's the idea behind Everyday Saints.

Faith isn't confined to Sunday worship, Church meetings, or formal religious study. It is something people live, question, discuss, learn about, and put into practice every day.

The name also reflects the people whose contributions make this website possible: individuals sharing their knowledge, experiences, research, testimony, creativity, and perspectives with others.

Not everyone featured here will agree on every subject. That's part of what makes exploring different voices worthwhile.

What You'll Find Here

Everyday Saints brings together content from a growing collection of creators and publishers.

Our goal is to make it easier to discover material across a variety of subjects and sources without requiring visitors to search through numerous individual websites and channels.

We don't produce or own the material featured in our feed. Instead, we help visitors find content published by its original creators, with links back to their work.

As the website develops, we hope to make discovering and exploring that content even easier.

An Independent Project

Everyday Saints is independently operated. It is not an official website of, sponsored by, or affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

The inclusion of a creator, publication, or particular piece of content does not necessarily constitute an endorsement of every opinion, interpretation, or claim expressed.

Our purpose is to help people discover material, not to dictate what they should think about it.

A Work in Progress

Everyday Saints is an ongoing project. We expect our collection of sources and the tools available to visitors to grow and improve over time.

While we include official Church channels and content, our primary focus is on everyday people doing their part to share the gospel, strengthen faith, explore ideas, and contribute to the wider Latter-day Saint community.

Occasionally, we may also feature content from people who are not members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints when we believe their contributions offer something of value to our visitors.

Please be patient with us as we grow! There are many, many blogs, channels, podcasts, and other resources out there. Finding them takes time, and adding them takes time, too.

Eventually, we'd also like to introduce more ways for visitors to participate and help Everyday Saints grow.

For now, it's line upon line, milk before meat. (Although, truthfully, there's already quite a bit of meat here!)

You can visit What's Next for announcements about the site's development, or explore Sources to learn more about the creators and publishers included here.
