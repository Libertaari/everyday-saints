Privacy Policy

Everyday Saints

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Everyday Saints ("we," "our," or "the website") respects the privacy of its visitors.

Everyday Saints is an independently operated website designed to help visitors discover Latter-day Saint-related videos, podcasts, articles, and other content published by a variety of creators and organizations.

We believe visitors should be able to explore this content without being required to create an account, provide personal information, or have their individual browsing activity tracked by us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit Everyday Saints, how that information is handled, and the role of third-party services.

2. Information We Collect

Everyday Saints does not require visitors to register, log in, or provide personal information to browse the website.

We do not independently maintain visitor profiles, personal browsing histories, or records of individual searches.

Our search and topic-filtering features are designed to operate without creating permanent visitor records.

However, certain technical information may be processed automatically by services that help operate and deliver the website, as described below.

3. Website Hosting and Analytics

Everyday Saints uses Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery, security, and traffic analytics.

Cloudflare may process technical information associated with website visits, including IP addresses, browser information, device characteristics, operating systems, approximate geographic locations, and requests made to the website.

Cloudflare provides us with aggregated statistics, such as visitor countries, browser types, operating systems, traffic volume, and website performance.

We use this information to understand general website activity, maintain reliable service, and identify technical problems.

We do not use these statistics to create individual visitor profiles.

For more information, visit Cloudflare's Privacy Policy .

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Everyday Saints does not independently use cookies to maintain visitor accounts, save browsing histories, or build advertising profiles.

Some third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for security, performance, advertising, or other purposes.

The use of these technologies depends on the services involved and their applicable settings.

Where required by applicable law, visitors will be provided with appropriate information and choices concerning nonessential cookies and similar technologies.

5. Advertising

Everyday Saints may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, and, where permitted, personalize advertising based on information collected across websites and services.

Everyday Saints does not independently create or maintain advertising profiles of its visitors.

Google's advertising practices are governed by its own policies and applicable legal requirements. Everyday Saints remains responsible for meeting the disclosure and consent obligations applicable to its use of advertising services.

Visitors can learn more through:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google's Advertising Information
  • Google's Ad Settings

6. Third-Party Content and External Links

Everyday Saints features content originating from independent creators, publishers, organizations, and other third parties.

Our website may provide titles, descriptions, images, and links directing visitors to the original content or publishing platform.

When you follow an external link, you leave Everyday Saints and become subject to the privacy practices and policies of the destination website.

These destinations may include YouTube, podcast platforms, blogs, and other websites.

Everyday Saints does not control the information collected by these external services and is not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of websites they choose to visit.

7. Email Communications

Visitors may contact Everyday Saints at contact@esaints.org .

When you send us an email, we receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message.

Email sent to our domain is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a dedicated Gmail account operated by Google.

We use correspondence to respond to questions, address concerns, consider corrections, and conduct ordinary website administration.

We do not sell contact information or use visitor correspondence to create marketing profiles.

Email communications may be retained as reasonably necessary for correspondence, recordkeeping, security, or legal purposes.

8. Information Sharing

Everyday Saints does not sell visitor personal information.

We may rely on third-party service providers, including Cloudflare and Google, to support website hosting, email communications, and advertising.

These providers may process information in connection with the services they provide, subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and legal obligations.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the security and operation of the website.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and proper operation of Everyday Saints.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Because Everyday Saints does not maintain visitor accounts or individual browsing histories, we do not operate a corresponding database of visitor account information.

Technical records and email communications may be retained by our service providers according to their respective practices and retention policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Everyday Saints provides general religious and educational content and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in a manner requiring deletion under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the matter.

Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact us using the address below.

11. Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may provide rights concerning the collection, use, access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

Visitors may also manage certain cookies and advertising preferences through their browsers and the privacy controls offered by relevant third-party services.

Because Everyday Saints does not maintain visitor accounts or individual browsing profiles, we may not possess identifiable records associated with a particular visitor.

For questions or requests concerning information we directly control, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Everyday Saints may update this Privacy Policy as the website develops, new features are introduced, or applicable requirements change.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Information

Questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to:

Everyday Saints
Email: contact@esaints.org
